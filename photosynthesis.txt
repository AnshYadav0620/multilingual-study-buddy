Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants make food. Plants take in carbon dioxide from the air through stomata and water from the soil through roots. Chlorophyll in the leaves absorbs sunlight. Using this light energy, the plant converts carbon dioxide and water into glucose and releases oxygen. The process takes place in the chloroplasts. The equation is: 6CO2 + 6H2O + light energy -> C6H12O6 + 6O2.
